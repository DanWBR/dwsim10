Version 10.2.9

- [NEW] Column Internals tool on the Utilities menu of both interfaces, also as a utility attached to a column: rates sieve, valve (Klein or the Glitsch Bulletin 4900 procedure) and bubble-cap (Bolles or modified Dauphine, rectangular or trapezoidal slots) trays (flooding, pressure drop, weeping, entrainment, downcomer backup, O'Connell efficiency) and random and structured packings (pressure drop, holdup, HETP, bed height; Rocha, Bravo and Fair for structured packings) of a solved rigorous column stage by stage, sizes each section for a target fraction of flood, writes the pressure profile, the efficiencies, the number of stages of a packed bed (bed height / HETP), the diameter and the heights into the column, keeps the case in the column, iterates with the solver, and marks the packed stages so the dynamic column runs them on the bed pressure drop and holdup correlations
- [NEW] Rate-based mode of the rigorous column: the Murphree efficiency of every component on every stage from mass transfer (AIChE or Chan-Fair transfer units and the Gerster mixing relation on trays, the HETP of each component on packed stages, Fuller and Wilke-Chang diffusivities), settled by solve-rate-solve passes on the Wang-Henke and Naphtali-Sandholm solvers, reported in the properties profile, with a packing catalogue and case files; checked against the worked examples of Towler and Sinnott, Seader and Henley and Ludwig
