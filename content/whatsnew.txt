Version 10.2.14

- [NEW] 3D plant model (CAD Tools > Export 3D Plant Model): builds the flowsheet in SolidCraft, a free CAD program installed separately, with equipment sized from its data or its streams, 2:1 or torispherical heads, platforms and ladders on tall columns, control valves, hand valves, PSVs, transmitters and indicators with their tags, lines sized and run between flanged nozzles with elbows and over a pipe rack, rerouted around items and other lines, steel finishes, insulation with aluminium cladding on hot and cold lines, operating conditions as properties and 3D labels, a flow animation and a replay of the last dynamic run (levels, valve openings and line variables on gauges), units with no model of their own drawn from their dimensions; the model is saved next to the simulation as a SolidCraft design, a STEP file, a picture, a photorealistic rendering and a web page that shows the model in any browser, a line list, a material take-off with a pressure drop screening of each route and a clash list (lines through items, lines that meet, items that overlap) are written next to it, items moved or turned in SolidCraft keep their place on the next export (saved with the simulation, CAD Tools > Reset 3D Plant Layout to start over), CAD Tools > Update 3D Plant Model Data sends new operating data to the model open in SolidCraft without building it again, and the settings hold the SolidCraft folder and what the model draws (labels, animation, insulation, platforms, instruments, dynamic replay, rendering, rack height, item gap)
- [NEW] Process flow diagram (CAD Tools > Export Process Flow Diagram): draws the flowsheet in CADCraft, a free CAD program installed separately, with equipment symbols, the streams routed nozzle to nozzle, horizontal and vertical and clear of the items, with their numbers, a stream table in the flowsheet units and a title block in the ISO, ASME or Petrobras standard, saved as DXF, DWG, PDF and PNG
- [NEW] P&ID (CAD Tools > Export P&ID): draws the flowsheet in CADCraft with line numbers, ISA control loops (transmitters, controllers with their set points, signal lines and control valves), local indicators, relief valves with their set pressure, alarms, interlocks from switches, nozzle marks, utility lines and a legend sheet, saved as DXF, DWG, PDF and PNG
- [NEW] Equipment drawings (CAD Tools > Export Equipment Drawings): a general arrangement drawing of each vessel, column, tank, exchanger, pump and compressor in CADCraft, with front and plan views to scale, main dimensions, nozzle schedule and item data, saved as DXF, DWG, PDF and PNG
- [NEW] Piping isometrics (CAD Tools > Export Piping Isometrics): one CADCraft drawing per line of the 3D model, with leg lengths, elevations, end coordinates, line data, preliminary design conditions, numbered welds and spools and a bill of materials, saved as DXF, DWG, PDF and PNG
- [NEW] Data sheets (CAD Tools > Export Data Sheets): one CADCraft sheet per item of equipment, control loop and local indicator, with operating and process data, preliminary design conditions, materials, dimensions, nozzles, exchanger TEMA type and geometry, and control valve sizing, saved as DXF, DWG, PDF and PNG
- [NEW] Plot plan (CAD Tools > Export Plot Plan): the 3D model's equipment from above in CADCraft, with coordinates, pipe rack, access road, battery limit, north arrow and equipment location table, saved as DXF, DWG, PDF and PNG
- [NEW] CAD Tools menu: the 3D plant model and the CADCraft drawings move to a menu of their own; each item opens a dialog with the SolidCraft or CADCraft folder found (another can be chosen), the output folder or drawing file and the tool's options, including the resolution and samples of the rendered picture
