Version 10.2.17

- [NEW] Reservoir Coupling (Patreon, Premium+; Tools > Reservoir Coupling (OPM Flow)): runs a reservoir simulation deck with OPM Flow together with the Pipe Network and the plant of the flowsheet; each report step the reservoir gives the well rates and the network the wellhead pressures, and streams of the flowsheet can set injection rates, group production limits and lift gas; the window installs OPM Flow (through WSL on Windows) and generates the black-oil PVT and the lift tables of the deck from the fluid of the flowsheet
- [NEW] PVT Analysis (Patreon, Premium and Premium+; Tools > PVT Analysis): characterizes a reservoir fluid from its laboratory PVT report with a cubic equation of state; splits and lumps the C7+ fraction, simulates the saturation pressure, constant composition expansion, differential liberation, constant volume depletion, separator and swelling tests next to the measured data, fits the heavy fractions by regression, calculates oil and gas viscosities (Lohrenz-Bray-Clark or corresponding states) and the minimum miscibility pressure with an injection gas, sends the fluid to the flowsheet (compounds, property package, stream) and exports a compositional model or black-oil tables for reservoir simulators
- [NEW] Solubility and Solvent Screening (Patreon, Premium+; Utilities > Solubility and Solvent Screening): solubility of a solid solute in the compounds of the flowsheet with the selected property package; solvent ranking at a hot and a cold temperature with the cooling yield, solubility curves, antisolvent study, ICH Q3C(R9) class and limit of each solvent with a filter, measured solubilities compared with the model, .dwsol case files; fit of NRTL parameters to measured solubilities
- [NEW] Crystallizer: Solubility Source option; with Property Package the saturation comes from the solid-liquid equilibrium of the package in the liquor as it is, the crystals leave as a solid phase and the duty includes the heat of crystallization; new results (supersaturation ratio, saturation temperature of the liquor, crystals in the slurry)
- [NEW] User Guide: a Reservoir Coupling part
- [NEW] User Guide: a PVT Analysis part
- [NEW] User Guide: a Solubility and Solvent Screening part
- [NEW] User Guide: a Lyophilizer part
- [NEW] COSMO-SAC property package (Patreon, Premium+): liquid phase activity coefficients predicted from sigma profiles, without binary interaction parameters; LVPP GMHB1808 parametrization and sigma-profile database (1,755 molecules)
- [NEW] Compounds: solid forms (polymorphs), a list of crystalline forms with their fusion data in the compound editor; the Crystallizer and the Solubility and Solvent Screening tool calculate with any form of the list and report the stable one
- [NEW] Compounds: heat capacity difference of fusion (liquid minus solid heat capacity at the melting point), used by the solid-liquid equilibrium; zero by default
- [NEW] Additional compounds: aspirin, paracetamol, 4-aminophenol, ibuprofen, the two intermediates of the ibuprofen synthesis (4-isobutylacetophenone and 1-(4-isobutylphenyl)ethanol) and four of its by-products
- [NEW] Lyophilizer unit operation (Patreon, Premium+): freeze drying of aqueous solutions in vials, with the time of the freezing, primary drying and secondary drying steps, the product temperature against the collapse temperature, the capacity of the freeze dryer, cycle charts and an operating map
- [CHG] Flowsheet Check: warns when the streams of one fluid around a unit use different property packages
- [CHG] Fail-safe flash with solids: a warning in the log names the stream, the specification and the original error
- [CHG] Windows installers of final releases are signed with a code signing certificate
- [CHG] Naphtali-Sandholm column solver: the bubble point pass that prepares its estimates solves the column once and leaves a composition or stream ratio specification to the Newton iteration; it used to search for the reflux or bottoms rate that met it, which could take longer than the solve itself
- [FIX] Pipe Segment and Pipe Network with Beggs and Brill: the two-phase friction factor used the wrong branch of the correlation in part of its range, with friction about 3.4 times too high in one band and jumps near another
- [FIX] Pipe Network: the nodal solver could not raise its damping factor again once it fell below 0.3 and ran to the iteration limit
- [FIX] Excess enthalpy of NRTL, UNIQUAC, UNIFAC, Modified UNIFAC, NIST-MFAC and Wilson (Excess and Experimental Liquid + Excess modes) was too large where the activity coefficients are far from one
- [FIX] Liquid viscosity: a compound above its critical temperature without a Henry's constant stopped the calculation with an error
- [FIX] Enthalpy and entropy of fusion away from the melting point used the solid heat capacity alone; the enthalpy of fusion could turn negative and PH flashes with solids landed on wrong temperatures
- [FIX] Solid-liquid-vapor PT flash could stop at a wrong vapor fraction; the mixture entropy left out the solid phase
- [FIX] Solid-liquid-vapor PT flash ran to its iteration limit over a narrow temperature range where the solvent boils with solid present
- [FIX] PH flash with solids: a partially vaporized mixture lost its solid phase in the final state and came out at a wrong temperature
- [FIX] Solid-liquid flash ran to its iteration limit where the activity coefficient model is close to a liquid-liquid split
- [FIX] Activity coefficient and ideal packages: a compound dissolved in a liquid below its own melting point took the vapor pressure of the solid, which made it less volatile than it is (water in cold methanol or glycol solutions, for example)
- [FIX] Solid fraction flash in solid solution mode read the specification as a liquid fraction and crashed with three or more compounds
- [FIX] Sulfur and Iodine (ChEDL): the extra elements uS and uI broke the element matrix of the Gibbs reactor; the vapor pressure curve of Sulfur was unusable
- [FIX] Fusion data of salicylic, adipic, citric and ascorbic acid
- [FIX] Joback estimation from a structure skipped 22 of the 41 groups without a message
- [FIX] Flowsheet Check listed objects on a package other than the first as solved with another property package right after opening a file
- [FIX] Spreadsheet: formulas referring to a worksheet stored later in the file were lost when the flowsheet was opened
- [FIX] Scripts: the ideal gas heat capacity of a ChEDL compound requested with the compound object was 1000 times the molar mass too small
- [FIX] Filter: a feed whose solid compounds don't have a solid density gave a filter area that was not a number; the block now stops with a message that names the compounds
- [FIX] Bubble and dew point of a mixture with one compound above 99.999 mol %: the K-values returned were those of the starting temperature or of the estimates, so a column stage of nearly pure water could keep K = 0.78 for water and the bubble point column solver stopped on a profile that did not close the mass balance
- [FIX] Bubble point (Wang-Henke) column solver: with a distillate rate at the condenser, in moles or in mass, and a boil-up ratio, composition, recovery or temperature specification at the reboiler, the solver searched for the bottoms rate, which the distillate rate already fixes, and could run without finishing; it now searches for the reflux ratio, also with side draws or a partial condenser
- [FIX] Naphtali-Sandholm column solver: with a total condenser, a column whose reflux had to move far from the estimates could run without finishing (benzene and toluene with a distillate rate and a boil-up ratio); the balance of the condenser stage is now linear in the solver variables
- [FIX] Bubble and dew pressure at a given temperature, and the saturation flashes of the Nested Loops (second version) and Nested Loops (SLE) algorithms: with one compound above 99.999 mol % they returned K-values that didn't belong to the result (0.46 for water at its boiling point)
- [FIX] Distillation and absorption columns with a property package that handles solids in the default flash: the column stopped with an index out of range error while preparing its estimates when a solid was present at the feed conditions
- [FIX] Default flash with solids, mixtures of an alcohol and a hydrocarbon: a liquid supersaturated in a solid could come out without the solid (a slurry returned as a clear liquid), and a wet cake under vacuum without vapor
- [FIX] Default flash with solids, mixtures with a compound without a vapor pressure correlation (a biomass compound, a sugar or an acid of the food properties database): as soon as another compound passed its saturation, the whole mixture, water included, was returned as a solid
- [FIX] Naphtali-Sandholm column solver with a partial condenser: the column takes its vapor product at the rate set on it and solves for the liquid distillate; with a product rate or a reflux ratio at the condenser it stopped with "Error evaluating error functions"
- [FIX] Naphtali-Sandholm column solver: a column with a side draw at a set rate, a distillate rate and a boil-up ratio could run without finishing; the line search now accepts a step that reduces the residual measured with each equation on its own scale
- [FIX] Columns with a reflux ratio and a boil-up ratio: the starting distillate rate was half of the feed whatever the two ratios; it now comes from the two ratios, and an ethanol and water column that took 38 s with Naphtali-Sandholm and more than 90 s with the bubble point solver takes 3 s and 6 s
- [FIX] Cake Filter: a feed without solids stopped the filter with an invalid composition error; the filtrate is now the feed and the cake stream is empty
- [FIX] Cake Filter: a trace of vapor in the feed, under 0.1 % of its mass (a dissolved gas at its bubble point), stopped the filter; it now leaves with the filtrate
- [FIX] Bio Reactor in Batch and Fed-Batch modes: the outlet was the feed scaled to the reactor volume over the batch duration, so its mass flow differed from the inlet whenever the two didn't match; the outlet now keeps the mass flow of the inlet and a warning reports the mismatch
- [FIX] Material streams without flow that hold a solid phase: the overall enthalpy came out invalid, and the unit operation that received the stream stopped
- [FIX] Gas-Liquid Separator: with inlets without flow it stopped with an undefined composition; it now takes the composition and conditions of its inlets and returns empty products
