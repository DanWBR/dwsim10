- [NEW] 3D plant model (Tools > Export 3D Plant Model): builds the flowsheet in SolidCraft, a free CAD program installed separately, with equipment sized from its data or its streams, 2:1 or torispherical heads, platforms and ladders on tall columns, lines sized and run between flanged nozzles with elbows and over a pipe rack, steel finishes, insulation with aluminium cladding on hot and cold lines, operating conditions as properties and 3D labels, and a flow animation; the model is saved next to the simulation as a SolidCraft design, a STEP file and a picture, a line list, a material take-off with a pressure drop screening of each route and a clash list (lines through items, lines that meet, items that overlap) are written next to it, items moved or turned in SolidCraft keep their place on the next export (saved with the simulation, Tools > Reset 3D Plant Layout to start over), and the SolidCraft folder can be set in the settings
