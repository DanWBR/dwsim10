Version 10.2.10

- [NEW] Operator Training Simulator extender in the cross-platform interface (Operator Training menu on Windows, Linux and macOS, Patreon level 3), with the same instructor station, operator panel, operator screens, alarms, interlocks, scenarios and OPC UA server as the Classic one
- [NEW] Techno-Economic Analysis: the case (setup, cost percentages, economic parameters and the edits on the equipment, material and utility maps) is saved with the flowsheet and restored, with the analysis run again, when the tool is opened; the automation API reads the same stored edits
- [NEW] Embedded charts on the flowsheet for more unit operations: a chart object bound to the object offers the curves of the bioreactor, the anaerobic digester (ADM1 trajectory), the CFB pyrolysis reactor (riser profiles), the chromatography column (breakthrough, load), the crossflow ultrafiltration (flux, VCF, concentrations), the heat exchanger (T-Q), the pump, compressor and expander (curves and maps of every speed with the operating point), the pipe network (profile of every solved pipe), the polymerization reactor (molecular weight distribution), the copper bed and zeolite adsorbers (breakthrough curve, PSA cycle pressure), the PEM fuel cells (output curves against the current), the ion exchange column (effluent against bed volumes, by sweeping the model) and the water electrolyzer (efficiency and hydrogen production against the cell voltage)
- [NEW] Refining Blender: octane (linear, Ethyl RT-70 or user coefficients) and RVP (linear or Chevron index) blending on a volume or mass basis, with the blended RON, MON, AKI, sensitivity and RVP on the outlet; Average pressure mode mass-weighted; settings saved with the flowsheet
- [CHG] Compressor in dynamic mode is a pressure-flow element by default, as the pump is; the casing capacity comes back with Integrate Casing Holdup; Rated Speed declared on the compressor
- [CHG] Heater and cooler in dynamic mode: the efficiency scales the duty that reaches the fluid, as it does at steady state (nothing changes at the default 100 %)
- [CHG] Tutorial Fluent API example scripts run under pythonnet as published (bootstrap, Q.* quantities, Plus tools from DWSIM_PATRON_KEY), and the pages that quote them were resynced
- [CHG] Flowsheet Check: PUMP_VAPOR_INLET (a pump fed with no liquid stops; a partly vapour feed is a warning) and PROPERTY_PACKAGE_CHANGED (results computed with a property package that was since replaced)
- [FIX] Gibbs reactor in adiabatic mode: the phase split inside the Gibbs minimization was computed at the starting temperature and never refreshed, so a reactor solved for the first time settled on a composition that is not an equilibrium (water-gas shift at 600 K and 10 atm: 910 K and 79 % CO conversion against 827 K and 56 % from the equilibrium reactor); the loop now follows the trial temperature, starts from the inlet temperature and stops with a message after 100 passes
- [FIX] Gibbs reactor: the first solve of a new reactor stopped on "invalid initial estimates" and only the second attempt ran; the feed is the starting point when there is no stored solution
- [FIX] Equilibrium reactor in adiabatic mode: a fresh reactor with no outlet temperature estimate evaluated the equilibrium constant at 0 K and stopped on "evaluated to infinity"; the inlet temperature is the starting point
- [FIX] Recycle block: in the default (non-legacy) mode a tear stream of a single compound never converged (the outlet was flashed at pressure and the enthalpy it happened to carry, so it never met the inlet), and the Wegstein and dominant eigenvalue accelerations chosen in the block were ignored; enthalpy and entropy now travel with the temperature, Wegstein acts on every tear variable within Qmin/Qmax on the block's schedule, the dominant eigenvalue is estimated from two consecutive plain passes, and the first pass of a solve is plain substitution
- [FIX] Relief valve in dynamic mode: the opening reached the Kv curves as a fraction where they expect a percentage, so a fully open valve passed about 1 % of its orifice capacity; the choked-flow test was inverted and the choked formula carried the API 520 exponent upside down; a fully open valve now passes the API 520 critical flow and the vessel it protects stays inside the lift band
- [FIX] Techno-Economic Analysis: typed prices, chosen materials and utilities, correction factors and comments were lost on Update All, and a typed price still received the cost-index correction; edits now survive, a typed price counts as current money, every price edit re-runs the costs, cash flow and report, and Reset Defaults rebuilds from the databases
- [FIX] Compressor surge alarm compared the inlet flow against a design flow the steady state never recorded; the design inlet volumetric flow is now recorded (read-only dynamic property) and the alarm fires below the surge flow fraction of it
- [FIX] Cooler in dynamic mode: the heat removed was applied to the cooler contents as heat added, so a cooler with a positive duty warmed its outlet and a temperature controller on it ran away; the duty now leaves the contents and the outlet settles at the steady-state temperature
- [FIX] Heater in dynamic mode: the Heat Added/Removed calculation mode ran with no duty at all, and Energy Stream mode with no energy stream connected crashed instead of reporting the missing stream
- [FIX] Custom Mixer sample: in Inlet Average mode the script divided the sum of the inlet pressures by the number of inlets minus one (three inlets at 1.01, 10.13 and 7.5 bar gave 9.32 bar for an average of 6.21 bar); the outlet now leaves at 6.21 bar and 269.5 K
- [FIX] CSTR in dynamic mode: a reactor stable at steady state ignited when the run started, at a time that depended on the integration step; inventory and extent in one basis, reaction heat over the step, empty holdup handled, conversions between 0 and 100 %
- [FIX] Compressor, pump and expander in dynamic mode: the outlet is specified by pressure and enthalpy, so the compression enthalpy is no longer discarded by the outlet flash
- [FIX] Corrosion and Scaling Monitor: CO2 corrosion rate about a thousand times too low (mass-transfer resistances in series), remaining life capped at 100 years instead of overflowing, remaining life and inhibitor dosing written by the pipe and exchanger runs, SSC Region 2/3 boundary per ISO 15156-2
- [FIX] Shortcut refining units: TEA and LCA recognise every unit (matched by class), the TEA sizes each in the units of its correlation, surrogate compound names match regardless of case, and the unit settings are saved with the flowsheet
- [FIX] PSV Sizing tool: the relieving pressure was formed as absolute and then treated as gauge, adding one atmosphere twice; explicit Relieving Pressure field and absolute or gauge labels
- [FIX] TEA and LCA defaults: water streams no longer priced as product, reboiler duties as steam and condenser duties as cooling water, exchangers without a computed area flagged, compressor correlation kept in range; LCA emissions only on outlets leaving the flowsheet and a product stream as the functional unit
- [FIX] Dynamic column: a valve on a drained reflux drum or sump passes nothing
- [FIX] Dynamic properties set from a script survive a stored flowsheet state
- [FIX] FluentAPI: the stream builder zeroes every compound a feed never names; the ammonia, ethanol and methanol tutorial samples regenerated with the tutorial feed
- [FIX] Files saved from the API carried no build version, so the loader switched off the skip-equilibrium option and re-flashed every unit-operation product with a trace of the other phase
- [FIX] Pump: a feed with no liquid stops with an error instead of solving with zero power
- [FIX] NRTL, UNIQUAC and Wilson: a failed estimation of missing interaction parameters is reported as a warning instead of running the pair as ideal in silence
- [FIX] Separator: an outlet with no flow takes the vessel state and the feed composition instead of stale phase data
- [FIX] A stream whose composition is empty or undefined fails with a message; the fraction setters no longer divide by a zero sum
