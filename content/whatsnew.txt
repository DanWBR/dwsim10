Version 10.2.9

- [NEW] Column Internals tool on the Utilities menu of both interfaces, also as a utility attached to a column: rates sieve, valve (Klein or the Glitsch Bulletin 4900 procedure) and bubble-cap (Bolles or modified Dauphine, rectangular or trapezoidal slots) trays (flooding, pressure drop, weeping, entrainment, downcomer backup, O'Connell efficiency) and random and structured packings (pressure drop, holdup, HETP, bed height; Rocha, Bravo and Fair for structured packings) of a solved rigorous column stage by stage, sizes each section for a target fraction of flood, writes the pressure profile, the efficiencies, the number of stages of a packed bed (bed height / HETP), the diameter and the heights into the column, keeps the case in the column, iterates with the solver, and marks the packed stages so the dynamic column runs them on the bed pressure drop and holdup correlations, with a packing catalogue and case files; checked against the worked examples of Towler and Sinnott, Seader and Henley and Ludwig
- [NEW] Rate-based mode of the rigorous column: the Murphree efficiency of every component on every stage from mass transfer (AIChE or Chan-Fair transfer units and the Gerster mixing relation on trays, the HETP of each component on packed stages, Fuller and Wilke-Chang diffusivities), settled by solve-rate-solve passes on the Wang-Henke and Naphtali-Sandholm solvers, applied as computed when above 1, with stripped trace components kept positive, reported in the properties profile with the transfer units and Peclet number of every tray
- [NEW] Colour streams by property (View menu of both interfaces): every solved material stream and the lines attached to it take a colour from the value of its temperature, pressure, vapour fraction, phase (liquid, vapour, two-phase, solid) or mass flow, with a scale in the corner of the flowsheet; the choice is saved with the simulation, and Solver Status brings the usual colours back
- [NEW] The finished flowsheet of every tutorial of the tutorials site (16 files, Mixer Basics to Polymerization Reactor) ships in the Samples list of the welcome screen, named Tutorial 01 to Tutorial 16, each with a title, a note on what the process does and what to try, a table of every stream (state, flows, composition), the results of the units that matter and the temperature and flow profiles of the columns
- [NEW] Explain Result tool (Flowsheet Analysis menu and the object's context menu in both interfaces, dwsim_explain_result on the MCP server): for a solved stream, separator, heater, cooler, heat exchanger, pump, compressor, expander, valve, mixer, splitter, reactor, shortcut column or rigorous column (distillation and absorbers), writes the balances and equilibrium relations it satisfied with the flowsheet's numbers substituted in, the reasoning a textbook would give (why the stream is all liquid, why the mixer outlet is not the average temperature, why the CSTR needs more volume than a PFR) and the diagram of the case: the Rachford-Rice function and the K values of a flash, the heating curve of a heater, the T-Q diagram with the pinch and the LMTD of an exchanger, the isenthalpic path of a valve, the isentropic reference of a compressor, the Levenspiel plot of a kinetic reactor, the Fenske, Underwood and Gilliland steps of a shortcut column, the balances, reflux, key pair with Fenske and Underwood and the temperature, flow and composition profiles of a rigorous column; the report can be copied as text
- [NEW] Site settings file for classrooms and companies: a dwsim.site.ini with the sections and keys of dwsim.ini (next to the application, at %ProgramData%\DWSIM or /etc/dwsim, or named by the DWSIM_SITE_INI environment variable) is laid over every user's settings on start, so the language, the solver mode, the inspector and the update check stay pinned; the Windows installers take /SITEINI="path" to install it, and the tutorials site documents the silent install and the offline documentation
- [NEW] Contextual help: F1 (Help menu, Help for the Selection) opens the tutorials page that explains the selected object, or the open teaching tool, or the Fundamentals track when nothing is selected, in the language of the interface; the tutorials site gained a Fundamentals track (streams and balances, degrees of freedom, recycles and convergence, choosing a thermodynamic model, units and numbers, common mistakes, the learning tools) in English and Portuguese
- [NEW] Live Sliders tool (Flowsheet Analysis menu of both interfaces): bind a specification of any object (only the ones its calculation mode reads are offered) to a slider, watch a few results while it moves, every position solved as it comes, the first watched result traced against the first slider so the response curve appears as you drag (a jump is a phase change, a flat stretch a saturated specification), with case files (.dwsld)
- [NEW] Scenario Comparison tool (Flowsheet Analysis menu of both interfaces; dwsim_scenario_snapshot and dwsim_scenario_compare on the MCP server): capture every property of every object after a solve, change a specification, solve and capture again, and read the two side by side: the specifications that changed come first (told apart from results by the degrees-of-freedom analysis of each object's calculation mode), then every result ordered by how much it moved, with the objects the change did not reach, a written summary and the report as text; snapshots are saved as .dwsnap files and can be compared with a later session
- [NEW] Property Package Comparison tool (Utilities menu of both interfaces): the T-x-y or P-x-y diagram of a binary drawn with every property package of the simulation (or the chosen ones), measured points laid over the curves (a dataset picked from the local phase-equilibrium database, or points typed in), each package scored by its mean and largest deviation on the bubble temperature or pressure and on the vapour composition, a ranking, the azeotropes each model sees or misses, and a written verdict on what the numbers mean (which packages sit within the data, why an equation of state without fitted interaction parameters misses a polar pair, what carrying the error into the flowsheet costs), with a deviation chart and case files (.dwppc)
- [NEW] McCabe-Thiele Diagram tool (Utilities menu of both interfaces): the equilibrium curve of a binary pair from the flowsheet's property package (bubble-point flashes, so the diagram reflects the thermodynamic model in use), the q-line, the operating lines, the pinch and the minimum reflux, the stages stepped off at the chosen reflux (as a value or as a multiple of the minimum) with a Murphree efficiency, the feed stage, the minimum number of stages at total reflux, azeotrope detection, the T-x-y diagram beside it, the inputs read off a selected shortcut column, a stage table, a text report and case files (.dwmct)
- [NEW] Equation of State Explorer tool (Utilities menu of both interfaces): the P-V isotherms of van der Waals, Redlich-Kwong, Soave-Redlich-Kwong or Peng-Robinson for one compound of the flowsheet, the three-root loop below the critical temperature with the Maxwell tie line at the saturation pressure the equation predicts, the compressibility factor against pressure, the saturation pressure of the equation beside the compound's vapour pressure correlation, the critical compressibility factor and volume of the equation beside the database values, a text report and case files (.dweos)
- [NEW] Flowsheet Check tool (Flowsheet Analysis menu of both interfaces, F8): lists what is wrong with the flowsheet before solving and what a real process could not have produced after solving (a heater that cooled its stream, a pump that lowered the pressure, a temperature cross in an exchanger, a shortcut column below its minimum reflux, a liquid below its freezing point, efficiencies typed as fractions, split ratios that do not sum to 1, a reactor with no reactions), with a written explanation of every code (what it means, why it happens, how to fix it) and a link to the tutorials; a Degrees of Freedom tab lists, per object, the specifications its calculation mode reads and which are missing; the same checks reach the assistant and the MCP server (dwsim_flowsheet_degrees_of_freedom, dwsim_explain_finding, explanations and learn-more links on every finding)
- [NEW] Dynamic rigorous column: quasi-steady vapor option (a stage keeps the vapor its free volume holds and passes the rest up within the step, stage pressures from the tray hydraulics top-down, the condenser drum at its bubble pressure), sub-steps of the integration step, holdup seeded at the level the weir passes the steady-state liquid rate with a half-full sump, tray coefficients calibrated to the steady-state pressure profile on request, stage temperatures and the sump level readable and monitorable
- [NEW] Fluent API: chart objects bound to a dynamics integrator, to a unit operation chart or to the chart collection, property tables, master tables, text notes and embedded pictures on the PFD (AddChart, AddPropertyTable, AddMasterTable, AddText, AddImage)
