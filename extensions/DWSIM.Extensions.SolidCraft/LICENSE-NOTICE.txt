DWSIM 3D plant model and drawings extension - third-party material
==================================================================

Resources/osifont.ttf
  osifont, a free TrueType font for CAD drawings after ISO 3098.
  Source: https://github.com/hikikomori82/osifont (file osifont.ttf, unmodified)
  License: GNU General Public License, version 3, with the GPL font exception
  (as stated in the osifont README for osifont.ttf).
  The extension embeds the font and writes it next to a process flow diagram,
  for the user to install, when it is not installed on the computer.

Resources/dexpi_identifiers.tsv
  The IUPAC name and the ChEBI identifier of the compounds of DWSIM's databases,
  by CAS number, written into the DEXPI models the extension exports.
  Source: PubChem (https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov), National Center for
  Biotechnology Information, U.S. National Library of Medicine, through its
  PUG REST service: the IUPAC name computed by PubChem for each compound and the
  ChEBI identifier it lists for it. PubChem data is in the public domain.
  The ChEBI identifiers are those of ChEBI, Chemical Entities of Biological
  Interest (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi), EMBL-EBI, licensed under the Creative
  Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Only the identifiers are used;
  they weren't changed.

SolidCraft and CADCraft are separate programs the user installs; the extension
only runs their command-line tools and links nothing of them.
