DWSIM AI Assistant launcher extension - licensing notice
=========================================================

This project is the *launcher/bridge* for the DWSIM AI Assistant. It is the thin
glue that DWSIM loads through its extension interface: it registers the menu item
and toolbar button, exposes the active flowsheet to a local HTTP API, starts and
stops the assistant's server process, and opens the assistant's web panel.

This launcher is part of DWSIM and is released under the GNU General Public
License, version 3 or later (see the DWSIM LICENSE). Because it links the DWSIM
engine (which is GPL), it is itself GPL, and its source is published with DWSIM.

The DWSIM Assistant server itself - the executable this launcher starts - is a
SEPARATE, INDEPENDENT program. It is not part of DWSIM, is not linked into it,
and communicates only at arm's length over a local HTTP connection. It is a
proprietary product distributed under its own license, and may be shipped
alongside the open edition as a separate component (mere aggregation under
GPLv3 section 5), or offered as a separate optional download.

In short:
  - launcher extension (this project) ......... GPL, open, part of DWSIM
  - assistant server executable (separate) .... proprietary, its own license
