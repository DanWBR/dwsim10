# Notes and limitations

- The dissolved salts never precipitate, so the burkeite and sodium carbonate scaling of the concentrators isn’t modeled.

- The heat of dilution comes from one slash pine liquor \[8\] and is applied to eucalyptus liquor too.

- The black liquor of the model has more carbon and a higher heating value (about 15.7 MJ/kg) than mill liquors (13.8 to 13.9 MJ/kg \[24\]), which carry chloride and potassium as dead load. The recovery boiler then raises more steam per kg of solids than a mill does.

- The package gives dissolved NaOH the formation enthalpy of the crystal, without a heat of solution, so the digester duty is about 0.3 GJ/ADt too low.

- Methanol, turpentine and the reduced sulfur gases aren’t produced by the digester.

- The extraction and peroxide bleaching stages, brightness and viscosity of the pulp aren’t modeled.
