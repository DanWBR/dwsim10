# Bauxite Digester

The bauxite and the liquor pass through stirred tanks in series at the digestion temperature, eight by default. In each tank:

- **Desilication:** the kaolinite reacts at first order to the desilication product, $\ce{3Al2Si2O5(OH)4 + 6NaOH + Na2CO3 -> Na8Al6Si6O24(CO3)*6H2O + 3H2O}$, which takes 1.18 kg of $\ce{Na2CO3}$ and 0.85 kg of $\ce{Al2O3}$ per kg of reactive silica. The rate constant comes from two anchors: 81.3 % of the kaolinitic silica in 22 h at 95 °C \[11\], and a rate ten times slower than gibbsite at 145 °C \[12\].

- **Gibbsite dissolution:** $\ce{Al(OH)3 + NaOH -> NaAl(OH)4}$, first order in the undissolved gibbsite with the driving force $(1-A/A^{*})$, and $k=8.72\times10^{7}\exp(-74990/RT)$ 1/s from Perić et al. \[13\].

Boehmite, the iron minerals, quartz and anatase stay in the mud. The block takes a residence time or a target desilication. The heat port takes the duty that brings the feed to the digestion temperature plus the heat of the reactions.
