# Building a refinery

The *Gibbsitic bauxite alumina refinery* case of the Case Library makes about 0.95 Mt/y with these blocks. The digested slurry and the pregnant liquor give their heat to the strong liquor through coolers and heaters that share an energy stream; heat exchangers would need more recycles. The NaOH make-up and the carbonate that the bauxite organics form were found with the strong liquor and seed loops open, and then the loops were closed.

The total energy agrees with the South American average. Digestion and evaporation take more than in a first-quartile refinery, because the case doesn’t recover the flash vapour below 120 °C and the evaporator doesn’t have condensate preheaters.
