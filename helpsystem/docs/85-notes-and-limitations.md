# Notes and limitations

- Pans and crystallizers are equilibrium stages: the mother liquor leaves saturated, where in a real pan it leaves supersaturated. Molasses purities from the model are the lower bound of what a pan house reaches.

- The impurity terms were fitted to Australian cane molasses. Beet molasses, with its different non-sugars, is outside the fit.

- Glucose and fructose stay dissolved; the package doesn’t crystallize them.

- The viscosity of impure molasses comes from the equation for sucrose solutions, applied to the total dissolved solids.

- Ethanol has a role in the model with the S-UNIFAC parameters of the original paper, without a fit to fermented solutions.
