# Physical properties

- **Heat capacity:** the dissolved solids take an apparent heat capacity $c_{p,DS}=1.5363+0.007586\,t$ kJ/(kg K), with $t$ in °C, fitted with the excess heat capacity of the model to the table of Simion et al. \[7\] for 10 to 90 % sucrose and 10 to 100 °C (within 1.5 %).

- **Density:** the dissolved solids take a partial specific volume of $0.61425+0.01914\,w$ cm$^{3}$/g, with $w$ their mass fraction in the liquid, fitted to the density table of sucrose solutions of Bates \[8\].

- **Viscosity:** the equation of Génotelle \[9\] for sucrose solutions, on the mass fraction of dissolved solids.

- **Thermal conductivity:** $k=k_{w}\left(1-0.54\,w\right)$, from the table of Simion et al. \[7\].
