# Solvents and solid

The compounds of the feed are divided into the solvents, which evaporate, and the solid, which stays. The solvents can be named in the **Solvents** field, separated by semicolons. With the field empty, the block takes as solvents the compounds whose vapor pressure at the wall temperature is above 1 % of the chamber pressure.

The boiling temperature of the liquid at the chamber pressure, its heat of vaporization and the composition of the vapor come from the property package. When the cake holds more than one solvent, the vapor is richer in the more volatile one, the liquid left changes along the drying and its boiling temperature rises.
