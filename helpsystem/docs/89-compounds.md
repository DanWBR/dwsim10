# Compounds

The wood and black liquor compounds are in the additional compounds folder, with the tag *Pulp*. Each one has a formula and a formation enthalpy, so that the cooking, bleaching and combustion reactions close their element and energy balances:







| Compound | Formula | Role |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Cellulose | $\ce{C6H10O5}$ | wood and pulp |
| Xylan | $\ce{C5H8O4}$ | wood and pulp (hardwood hemicelluloses) |
| Glucomannan | $\ce{C6H10O5}$ | wood and pulp (softwood hemicelluloses) |
| Hardwood lignin | $\ce{C9H_{7.82}O_{2.75}(OCH3)_{1.59}}$ | wood and pulp |
| Softwood lignin | $\ce{C9H_{9.2}O_{2.6}(OCH3)_{0.8}}$ | wood and pulp |
| Wood extractives | $\ce{C18H34O2}$ | wood |
| Wood acetyl groups | $\ce{C2H2O}$ | wood |
| Sodium hardwood and softwood lignate |  | dissolved lignin of the cook |
| Sodium glucoisosaccharinate | $\ce{C6H11NaO6}$ | dissolved carbohydrates |
| Sodium xyloisosaccharinate | $\ce{C5H9NaO5}$ | dissolved carbohydrates |
| Sodium lactate | $\ce{C3H5NaO3}$ | short hydroxy acids of the cook |
| Tall oil soap | $\ce{C18H33NaO2}$ | dissolved extractives |
| Sodium sulfide | $\ce{Na2S}$ | white liquor and smelt |
| Calcium oxide | $\ce{CaO}$ | lime |
| Chlorine dioxide | $\ce{ClO2}$ | bleaching (dissolved) |
| Dissolved hardwood and softwood lignin |  | lignin of the bleach plant |



The formation enthalpies of the wood compounds come from their heats of combustion (cellulose, hemicelluloses and lignin), and those of the dissolved salts from the acid and the neutralization by NaOH. Sodium sulfide and calcium oxide follow the NIST-JANAF tables \[19\]. Chlorine dioxide takes the enthalpy of the gas plus its heat of solution from the temperature dependence of its Henry’s law constant. The inorganic salts of the liquors (NaOH, $\ce{Na2CO3}$, $\ce{Na2SO4}$, NaCl, the potassium salts, $\ce{CaCO3}$ and $\ce{Ca(OH)2}$) come from the electrolyte database of DWSIM.
