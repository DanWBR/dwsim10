# Limits of the model

- The solvent that freezes is water. Mixtures with organic solvents are not covered.

- The product is in vials. Trays of bulk product are not modelled.

- One vial stands for the batch. Vials at the edge of a shelf, which receive more heat, are not distinguished.

- The solutes do not change the vapor pressure of the ice.

- In secondary drying the product is at the shelf temperature.
