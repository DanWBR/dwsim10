# Overview

The Contact Dryer is a vacuum dryer for wet cakes in which the heat comes from a heated wall and the solvent leaves as vapor to a condenser. It covers two kinds of equipment: dryers with an agitated bed, such as agitated filter-dryers, pan, paddle, conical and tumble dryers, and dryers with the bed at rest on heated plates, such as vacuum tray dryers. The block follows one batch from the wet cake to the target solvent content and reports the drying time, the temperature of the bed, the capacity of the equipment, its duties and the solvents left in the product.

The block is part of the Patreon edition, for Premium+ members. It appears in the object palette as **Contact Dryer**, in the Classic and in the Cross-Platform interfaces.
