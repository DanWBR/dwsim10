# Pulp Washer

The pulp and the wash liquor enter, and the washed pulp and the filtrate leave, both at the adiabatic mixing temperature. The fibre and the other solids leave with the washed pulp at the outlet consistency, less the fibre loss. Every dissolved compound meets the Nordén efficiency normalized to 10 % outlet consistency \[12\]:



\[
E_{10}=\frac{\ln\left[\frac{L_{0}}{L_{1}}\frac{X_{0}-Y_{1}}{X_{1}-Y_{2}}\right]}{\ln\left(1+DF/9\right)},\qquad DF=V_{2}-L_{1}
\]


where $L_{0}$ and $L_{1}$ are the liquors with the pulp at the inlet and the outlet, $V_{2}$ the wash liquor, and $X$ and $Y$ the concentrations in the pulp liquor and in the wash liquor or filtrate. The washer types set $E_{10}$ and the outlet consistency to typical values: continuous digester wash zone, pressure and atmospheric diffusers, wash press, pressurized and vacuum filters and drum displacers with one to four stages. With the published balance of a wash press of Foelkel \[13\], the block returns the filtrate of 9.64 t/t at 1050 ppm (published 9.65 t/t and 1050 ppm), and two washers of $E_{10}=4$ in counter-current reach $E_{10}=8$, as the Nordén model says.
