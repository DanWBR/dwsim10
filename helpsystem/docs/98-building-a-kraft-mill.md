# Building a kraft mill

The *Bleached eucalyptus kraft mill* case of the Case Library makes about 1460 ADt/d with these blocks: Kraft Digester, brown stock and post-oxygen washers, two Bleaching Stages in O mode and two in D mode with their washers, a press and a dryer, Evaporator Train, Recovery Boiler with the dissolving tank, Causticizer, lime mud washer and Lime Kiln. The white liquor and the reburned lime go back through recycles.

- **Make-ups:** the sodium and sulfur that the mill loses come back as salt cake ($\ce{Na2SO4}$, burned with the liquor) and NaOH. In the case, the make-ups and the mud wash water were found with the white liquor and the lime loops open, until the white liquor that the causticizer made matched the one the mill used; then the loops were closed. If the fibreline changes, the loops settle at another inventory of sodium and sulfur, so the make-ups should be found again.

- **Recycles:** a mass flow tolerance of 0.005 kg/s, above the numerical noise of the washers and the digester, and Wegstein acceleration.

- **Evaporation:** the Evaporator Train in one block; a train of Falling Film Evaporators in backward feed needs a recycle per effect.
