# Results

- **Cycle:** the time of freezing, primary drying and secondary drying, the time at which the product is completely frozen, and the cycle time with loading and unloading.

- **Product:** the thickness of the frozen layer, the highest product temperature in primary drying and its margin to the collapse temperature, the mean temperature of the sublimation front, the final moisture and the water content of the cake.

- **Batch and capacity:** the charge, the dried product and the water removed per batch, the capacity of one unit, the utilization with the feed of the flowsheet, the units required, and the peak sublimation rate per vial and for the batch.

- **Duties:** the refrigeration of the freezing step, the shelf heat of the two drying steps and the duty of the condenser, as averages of the cycle, and the peak duty of the condenser.

Two charts follow one vial along the cycle. **Cycle Temperatures** shows the shelf, the bottom of the vial and the sublimation front, with the collapse temperature. **Drying Rate and Moisture** shows the rate at which the water leaves and the moisture of the product. Both can be placed on the flowsheet as chart objects.

The **Operating Map** repeats primary drying over a grid of shelf temperatures and chamber pressures around the settings of the block, with both held from the start. Each point gives the drying time and the highest product temperature, and the points above the collapse temperature or above the equipment capability are marked. The map shows how far the shelf temperature can be raised, and which pressure gives the shortest drying for a product.
