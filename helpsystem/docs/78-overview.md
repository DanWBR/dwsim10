# Overview

The Sugar Solutions property package describes cane sugar solutions, from the juice that leaves the mills to the molasses that leaves the last centrifugal: water, sucrose, glucose, fructose and the impurities of the cane. It gives the boiling point elevation of juices and syrups, the solubility of sucrose in pure and impure solutions, the crystallization of sucrose in vacuum pans and crystallizers, and the density, viscosity, heat capacity and thermal conductivity that the evaporators, heaters and pans need.

The package is part of the Patreon edition, for Premium+ members. It appears in the list of property packages as **Sugar Solutions (S-UNIFAC)**, in the Classic and in the Cross-Platform interfaces. The Sugar Stream Analysis utility, described at the end of this part, reads a stream of a flowsheet in the quantities a sugar technologist uses.
