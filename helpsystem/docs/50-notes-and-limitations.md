# Notes and Limitations

- The sizes of the equipment and of the lines, the design conditions and the materials are preliminary estimates from the operating conditions: a starting point for the design.

- The line hydraulics screening only points at the lines that need a hydraulic calculation.

- The isometrics are not to scale, as isometrics are; the true length of every leg is written on it.

- The drawings follow the layout of the 3D model as DWSIM last read it. Read the places again after moving items in SolidCraft.

- The render times are estimated from one graphics card; the time on another one can be several times longer.
