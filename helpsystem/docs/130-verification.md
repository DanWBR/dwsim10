# Verification

The worked example of the original work \[1\] (its Appendix A: wall at 71.7 °C, 190 Pa, particles of 6 mm, mixing number 2) was run with the model. The contact coefficient is 34.23 W/(m<sup>2</sup>.K) against 34.27 in the example, with the roughness of the wall left out, and the drying rate at the start is 0.819 g/(m<sup>2</sup>.s) against 0.817. Along the curve the model stays below the rates of the example, by 3 % at half of the initial moisture and by 11 to 15 % under one tenth of it.

The agitated bed model was also run with the data of the industrial indirect dryer of copper concentrate simulated in \[2\]: 237 m<sup>2</sup> of heated area, wall at 186 °C, 4 rpm, mixing number 2.41 and a concentrate that goes from 10.5 % to 0.2 % of water.

| Quantity                                        | Reference \[2\] | Model  |
|:------------------------------------------------|:---------------:|:------:|
| Final temperature of the solid (°C)             |      148.2      | 149.1  |
| Drying rate at the inlet (kg/(m<sup>2</sup>.s)) |      0.015      | 0.0156 |
| Time in the dryer (s)                           |      2736       |  2384  |

The time is 13 % shorter with the solid flow that the reference states. The model has not been compared with a measured drying curve of a pharmaceutical product.
