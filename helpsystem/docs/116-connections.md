# Connections

| Port | Stream |
|:---|:---|
| Feed (inlet) | the liquid formulation, with water and the solutes |
| Dried Product (outlet) | the dried cake, with the solutes and the residual water |
| Vapor (outlet) | the water removed, as vapor at the chamber pressure |
| Shelf Heat (energy) | the heat supplied by the shelves in the two drying steps |

A freeze dryer is a batch unit, and the flowsheet is at steady state. The streams carry the average of the cycle: the feed is the solution that the batches consume over time, and the products are what they deliver. The block compares the feed with what one unit handles and reports how many units are required.
