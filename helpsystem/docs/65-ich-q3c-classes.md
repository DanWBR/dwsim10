# ICH Q3C classes

The ranking shows the class of each solvent in the guideline ICH Q3C(R9), *Impurities: guideline for residual solvents* (2024), and its concentration limit in the drug product:

Class 1:  
solvents that should be avoided, such as benzene and 1,2-dichloroethane.

Class 2:  
solvents to be limited, each with a concentration limit, such as methanol (3000 ppm), toluene (890 ppm) and hexane (290 ppm).

Class 3:  
solvents with low toxic potential, such as ethanol, acetone, ethyl acetate and heptane. The guideline accepts 5000 ppm of them without justification.

No data:  
solvents the guideline lists without adequate toxicological data.

A solvent that is not in the guideline shows a dash. Water is one of them. The field **Solvents kept (ICH Q3C)** keeps all the solvents, leaves out the ones of class 1, or keeps only the ones of class 3. The solvents left out are named in the Messages tab.
