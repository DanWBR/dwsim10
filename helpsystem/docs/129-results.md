# Results

- **Cycle:** the time to heat the bed to the boiling temperature, the drying time and the cycle time with loading and unloading.

- **Product:** the solvent in the wet cake and in the dried product, the boiling temperature of the liquid, and the highest and the final temperatures of the bed. The block warns when the bed passes the temperature limit of the product.

- **Heat transfer:** the contact coefficient, the mean overall coefficient of the batch, the depth of the bed and the peak drying rate.

- **Batch and capacity:** the charge, the product and the solvent removed per batch, the capacity of one dryer, its utilization with the feed of the flowsheet and the units required.

- **Duties:** the heat of the wall, as an average of the cycle and at its peak, and the duty of the condenser.

- **Solvents left in the product:** each solvent in ppm, with its class and its limit in ICH Q3C(R9). The block warns when a solvent is above its limit.

Two charts follow the batch: **Drying Curve**, with the solvent content of the bed, in total and by solvent, and the drying rate, and **Bed Temperature**, with the bed, the boiling temperature of the liquid and the wall. Both can be placed on the flowsheet as chart objects.

The **Operating Map** repeats the drying over a grid of wall temperatures and chamber pressures around the settings of the block, and gives the drying time and the highest bed temperature at each point.
