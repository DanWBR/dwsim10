# Compounds

The bauxite minerals and the dissolved salts that the databases don’t have are in the additional compounds folder, with the tag *Bayer*. Each one has a formula and a formation enthalpy, so that the digestion, desilication, precipitation, causticization and calcination reactions close their element and energy balances:







| Compound | Formula | Source of the formation enthalpy |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Boehmite | $\ce{AlO(OH)}$ | Robie and Hemingway \[1\] |
| Gamma alumina | $\ce{Al2O3}$ | NIST-JANAF \[2\] |
| Corundum | $\ce{Al2O3}$ | Robie and Hemingway \[1\] |
| Hematite | $\ce{Fe2O3}$ | Robie and Hemingway \[1\] |
| Goethite | $\ce{FeO(OH)}$ | Robie and Hemingway \[1\] |
| Kaolinite | $\ce{Al2Si2O5(OH)4}$ | Robie and Hemingway \[1\] |
| Quartz | $\ce{SiO2}$ | Robie and Hemingway \[1\] |
| Anatase | $\ce{TiO2}$ | Robie and Hemingway \[1\] |
| Desilication product (sodalite) | $\ce{Na8Al6Si6O24(CO3)*6H2O}$ | thermoneutral desilication |
| Sodium aluminate | $\ce{NaAl(OH)4}$ | heat of digestion of gibbsite \[3\] |
| Sodium oxalate | $\ce{Na2C2O4}$ | NBS 1982 \[4\] |
| Tricalcium aluminate (katoite) | $\ce{Ca3Al2(OH)12}$ | NBS 1982 \[4\] |



The minerals take the heat capacity tables of Robie and Hemingway, and gamma alumina that of NIST-JANAF. The heat capacities of the desilication product and of tricalcium aluminate are Neumann-Kopp estimates. Gibbsite, NaOH, the sodium salts, $\ce{Ca(OH)2}$ and $\ce{CaCO3}$ come from the electrolyte database of DWSIM.

With these compounds, the heat of digestion of gibbsite is 252.3 kJ/kg, and the calcination of gibbsite to gamma alumina and steam at 25 °C takes 1999 kJ/kg of $\ce{Al2O3}$, against 2011 kJ/kg in Raahauge \[17\].
