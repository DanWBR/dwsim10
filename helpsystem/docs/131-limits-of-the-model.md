# Limits of the model

- The dryer works under vacuum with the vapor of the solvents alone. A sweep of inert gas isn’t modelled.

- All the solvent is taken as free liquid. Solvent bound inside the particles, which leaves more slowly, isn’t modelled.

- The solid doesn’t change the boiling temperature of the liquid.

- The bed at rest doesn’t separate the solvents: its vapor has the composition of the liquid in the cake.

- Convective dryers, such as fluidized bed and spray dryers, are outside this block.
