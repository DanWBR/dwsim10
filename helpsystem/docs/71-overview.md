# Overview

The COSMO-SAC property package predicts the non-ideality of the liquid phase without binary interaction parameters. Each compound is described by its sigma profile, the distribution of electric charge that a conductor would show on the surface of the molecule, which comes from a quantum chemistry calculation made once per compound. The activity coefficients of a mixture are calculated from the profiles of its compounds alone.

The package is useful where group contribution methods lack groups or parameters and measured data are not available to fit NRTL or UNIQUAC, for instance when solvents are screened for a new molecule. It is a predictive model: its results show trends and orders of magnitude and do not replace measurements.

The package is part of the Patreon edition, for Premium+ members. It appears in the list of property packages as **COSMO-SAC (LVPP GMHB1808)**, in the Classic and in the Cross-Platform interfaces.
