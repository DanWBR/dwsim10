# Hydrate Calciner

The washed hydrate calcines to alumina with a set LOI, held as boehmite, and a set alpha fraction, which forms corundum; the rest is gamma alumina. The fuel burns with air at the wet $\ce{O2}$ of the exit gas, and the block writes the fuel flow that closes the heat balance of Raahauge \[17\]: the calcination and the evaporation of the moisture, the sensible heat of the exit gas and of the alumina that leaves the last cooling cyclone, and the surface loss, less the sensible heat of the hydrate, the air and the fuel. The heat port takes the duty of the fluid-bed cooler.

Against the balances of three gas suspension calciners \[17\], the sensible heat of the exit gas and the alumina is 4 to 9 % below the published values, which include the dust recycle and the air that leaks into the cyclones. The surface loss that remains is 145 to 258 kJ/kg (default 150).
