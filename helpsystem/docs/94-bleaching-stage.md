# Bleaching Stage

The pulp and a chemicals stream react at the stage temperature for the retention time, and the heat port takes the duty that brings the stage to its temperature. A Pulp Washer after the stage separates the filtrate.

**Chlorine dioxide stage (D):** the kinetics of Barroca, Simões and Castro for E. globulus kraft pulp \[14\]. In the first 15 s the kappa falls by $100\,(K_{0}-K_{\theta})/K_{0}=23.324+8.176\times10^{-6}e^{0.040\,T}$ (T in K), and then



\[
\frac{dK}{dt}=-k_{0}\,e^{-E_{a}/RT}\,[\ce{ClO2}]^{1.6}\,(K-K_{\infty})^{2.6},\qquad\frac{d[\ce{ClO2}]}{dt}=\alpha\,\frac{m}{V}\,\frac{dK}{dt}
\]


with \[$\ce{ClO2}$\] in mg/L, $t$ in min, $E_{a}=33.7$ kJ/mol, $\alpha=0.296\,(K_{0}-K)+0.866\,e^{0.035\,COD}$ mg/(g kappa), $K_{\infty}=33.880-0.0932\,T$ and $k_{0}=0.637$, fitted on the curves at 55 °C. The COD of the carry-over comes from the dissolved organics of the stream. The chlorine dioxide consumed oxidizes the removed lignin and then the other dissolved organics to $\ce{CO2}$ and water; the chloride leaves as NaCl and then as HCl.

**Oxygen stage (O):** the rate of Ji, Wheeler and van Heiningen \[16\], $r=3.47\times10^{3}\,e^{-46750/RT}\,[\text{NaOH}]^{0.60}\,P_{\ce{O2}}^{0.50}\,L_{c}$, with a rate factor of 0.619 fitted on the eucalyptus pulp of Segura et al. \[6\]. The removed lignin dissolves as sodium lignate, and the oxygen consumed (1.0 kg per kg of lignin by default) oxidizes it.

The extraction (E) and peroxide (P) stages aren’t in the block, and the D stage removes lignin only: the hexenuronic acids, which hot D stages also remove, stay in the pulp.
