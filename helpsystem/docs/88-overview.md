# Overview

The pulp and paper tools model a kraft pulp mill, from the wood chips to the bleached pulp and around the chemical recovery loop. They are part of the Patreon edition, for Premium and Premium+ members, in the Classic and in the Cross-Platform interfaces:

- **Kraft Liquors**, a property package for wood, pulp, white, green and black liquors, lime and lime mud;

- the **Pulp Stream Analysis** utility, which reads a stream in the quantities of a pulp mill and writes a stream from them;

- seven unit operations: **Kraft Digester**, **Pulp Washer**, **Bleaching Stage**, **Evaporator Train**, **Recovery Boiler**, **Causticizer** and **Lime Kiln**;

- the *Bleached eucalyptus kraft mill* case of the Case Library, which joins all of them.

The tools cover the kraft process only. Mechanical and recycled pulps and the paper machine aren’t modeled.
