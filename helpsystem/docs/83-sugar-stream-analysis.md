# Sugar Stream Analysis

**Utilities \> Sugar Stream Analysis** lists the material streams of the flowsheet that use the Sugar Solutions package and shows, for each one:

- **Brix**, from the dissolved solids and the water of the liquid, **pol** (from the specific rotations of sucrose, glucose and fructose), **true purity** and **apparent purity**;

- the **reducing sugars**, the **ash** and the **organic non-sugars** on dissolved solids, and the sucrose/water and impurity/water ratios;

- the **dry substance of a massecuite**, its **crystal content** and the **crystal yield**;

- the **insoluble solids** (fibre and mud);

- the sucrose/water ratio at **saturation** from the model, the **solubility coefficient** and the **supersaturation**;

- the **boiling point elevation** at the pressure of the stream.

The same window sets a stream from the quantities of the factory: its mass flow, Brix, true purity, the reducing sugars/ash and organic non-sugars/ash ratios, the share of glucose in the reducing sugars, and the fibre and mud contents. The composition is written to the stream and the flowsheet is solved again.
