# Phase equilibrium

The stability of a mixture is tested with the tangent plane analysis of Michelsen, from a vapour-like and a liquid-like trial phase. A two-phase flash starts with successive substitution, accelerated by the dominant eigenvalue method, and changes to Newton’s method when the convergence is slow, as it is near a critical point. The saturation pressure is the limit of the two-phase region found with the stability test, so it does not need to know beforehand whether the fluid has a bubble point or a dew point.
