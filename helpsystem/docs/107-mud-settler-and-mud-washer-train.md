# Mud Settler and Mud Washer Train

The **Mud Settler** splits a slurry into an overflow with a set solids content (g/L) and an underflow at a set solids content (%), with the same liquor in both. It also serves as a hydrate filter. The **Mud Washer Train** is a counter-current decantation train with a mixing efficiency per washer. It solves all the washers together and writes the outlets at the adiabatic temperature.

With ideal washers, the soda that stays with the mud equals the counter-current washing result $(R-1)/(R^{N+1}-1)$, with $R$ the wash ratio, to 1e-12. With the digester of the western Pará case and six washers at 2.8 t of wash water per t of mud \[14\], the mud factor is 1.36 t/t, inside the range of 0.7 to 2 t/t of the IAI \[15\], and the washers recover 98.9 % of the soluble soda. Reversion (gibbsite that precipitates on the mud in the washers) isn’t modeled; the blocks report the supersaturation instead.
