# Overview

The Lyophilizer is a freeze dryer for aqueous solutions in vials. A freeze dryer takes the water out of a product that cannot be heated: the solution is frozen in its vials, the ice sublimes under vacuum and the water left bound to the solid desorbs at a higher temperature. The block follows one vial through the three steps of the cycle, scales the result to the batch and reports the time of each step, the temperature of the product, the capacity of the equipment and its duties.

The block is part of the Patreon edition, for Premium+ members. It appears in the object palette as **Lyophilizer**, in the Classic and in the Cross-Platform interfaces.
