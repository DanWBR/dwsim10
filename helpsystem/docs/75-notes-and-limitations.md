# Notes and limitations

- The parametrization was fitted by LVPP to infinite dilution activity coefficients of organic mixtures. Small polar compounds diluted in water are its weakest point: their activity coefficients at infinite dilution come out too high.

- Electrolytes, ions, polymers and reacting systems are outside the model.

- Only hydrogen atoms bonded to oxygen count as strong hydrogen bond donors. Amines and amides as donors are described by the electrostatic term alone.

- A calculation costs more than with NRTL or UNIFAC. Flowsheets with columns take noticeably longer.

- The profiles of the database were made with one conformation of each molecule.
