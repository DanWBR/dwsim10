# Verification

With the published parameters for 5 % mannitol in 6R vials with 2 mL of fill, the shelves at -5 °C and the frozen volume of the reference, the model gives the primary drying times below.

| Chamber pressure (mTorr) | Reference \[1\] (h) | Model (h) |
|:------------------------:|:-------------------:|:---------:|
|           100            |        12.81        |   12.80   |
|           150            |        12.36        |   12.36   |
|           300            |        11.62        |   11.61   |
|           1500           |        15.84        |   15.80   |

For 5 % sucrose in the same vials the reference gives 36.64 h at 65 mTorr with the shelves at -30 °C and 24.88 h at 50 mTorr and -25 °C; the model gives 36.66 h and 24.89 h. In a flowsheet the times are 2 to 4 % longer, because the block includes the ramp of the shelves in primary drying and takes the density of the solution from the property package.
