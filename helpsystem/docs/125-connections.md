# Connections

| Port | Stream |
|:---|:---|
| Wet Cake (inlet) | the solid with the solvents it holds |
| Dried Product (outlet) | the solid with the solvents left |
| Vapor (outlet) | the solvents removed, saturated at the chamber pressure |
| Wall Heat (energy) | the heat supplied by the wall |

The dryer is a batch unit, and the flowsheet is at steady state. The streams carry the average of the cycle. The block compares the feed with what one dryer handles and reports how many units are required.
