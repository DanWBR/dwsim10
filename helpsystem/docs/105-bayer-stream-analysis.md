# Bayer Stream Analysis

The utility (Utilities menu) shows a stream as a refinery reads it: the liquor C, A, S, A/C, C/S and $\alpha_{c}$, the impurities and the TOC, the equilibrium alumina and the supersaturation, the boiling point, the solids per litre of slurry, the oxide analysis of the solids, and the available alumina and reactive silica of a bauxite. It also writes a stream from these quantities: a liquor, a bauxite (moisture and minerals), a seeded slurry or a red mud slurry, with presets from published data that the user should replace with the data of the refinery.
