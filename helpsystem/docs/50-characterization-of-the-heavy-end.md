# Characterization of the heavy end

#### Split

The molar mass $M$ of the heavy end follows the three-parameter gamma distribution


\[
p(M)=\frac{(M-\eta)^{\alpha-1}\exp\left[-(M-\eta)/\beta\right]}{\beta^{\alpha}\,\Gamma(\alpha)},\qquad\beta=\frac{M_{C7+}-\eta}{\alpha},
\]


where $\alpha$ is the shape, $\eta$ the lowest molar mass and $M_{C7+}$ the measured molar mass of the heavy end. The distribution is cut at intervals of 14 g/mol, one per carbon number, and the area and the average molar mass of each interval give the mole fraction and the molar mass of the fraction. The last fraction takes what remains, so the amount and the molar mass of the heavy end are preserved.

The specific gravity of each fraction comes from the correlation of Søreide,


\[
\gamma_{i}=0.2855+C_{f}\left(M_{i}-66\right)^{0.13},
\]


with the factor $C_{f}$ found so that the fractions together have the measured specific gravity of the heavy end. The boiling point of each fraction comes from the correlation of the same author.

#### Properties of the fractions

The critical temperature and pressure come from the correlation chosen in the window, as functions of the boiling point and the specific gravity. The acentric factor comes from the Lee-Kesler and Kesler-Lee correlations, and the critical volume from Hall-Yarborough.

#### Lumping

The fractions are lumped in groups of about the same mass. Each group takes the mole-fraction average of the critical temperature, the critical pressure, the acentric factor, the molar mass and the volume translation of its fractions.

#### Interaction parameters and volume translation

Nitrogen, carbon dioxide and hydrogen sulphide receive the usual constant interaction parameters with the hydrocarbons. Methane and each heavy fraction receive the parameter of the modified Chueh-Prausnitz rule,


\[
k_{ij}=A\left[1-\left(\frac{2\,v_{ci}^{1/6}v_{cj}^{1/6}}{v_{ci}^{1/3}+v_{cj}^{1/3}}\right)^{6}\right],
\]


with $A$ the methane interaction constant of the window. The other pairs of hydrocarbons do not have interaction parameters.

The volume translation subtracts a constant $c_{i}$ of each component from the molar volume of the equation of state, which corrects the liquid density without changing the phase equilibrium. The defined components have tabulated values. The translation of each heavy fraction is the one that reproduces its specific gravity at 60 °F.
