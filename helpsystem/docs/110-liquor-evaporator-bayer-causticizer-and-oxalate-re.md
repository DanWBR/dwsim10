# Liquor Evaporator, Bayer Causticizer and Oxalate Removal

- **Liquor Evaporator:** a multiple-effect evaporator in forward or backward feed. The liquor boils at the Dewey boiling point elevation, and the heat balances use the enthalpies of the package. The block finds the steam for a target caustic of the strong liquor. An effect whose heat doesn’t bring the liquor to boiling only heats it. In backward feed with a cold spent liquor, the train can have two operating states, and the block stops with a message.

- **Bayer Causticizer:** the fraction of the lime given by the lime efficiency (45 to 55 % in conventional causticizers \[19\]) turns sodium carbonate into NaOH. The rest of the lime forms tricalcium aluminate with the aluminate of the liquor, which takes 0.606 kg of $\ce{Al2O3}$ per kg of CaO. The block can write the lime flow for a target C/S.

- **Oxalate Removal:** sodium oxalate crystallizes down to its apparent solubility, the Beckham-Grocott solubility times a factor for the humates that poison the seed, and leaves in a filter cake with liquor.
